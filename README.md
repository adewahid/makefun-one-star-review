Entry for the [One-Star Review of Something Essential](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xb3e689-one-star-review-of-something-essential-c7234629) competition on make.fun.

The program writes a single deadpan one-star review (97 characters) of an everyday essential to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Gravity: works perfectly until the one second you need it not to, then bills you in broken glass.
